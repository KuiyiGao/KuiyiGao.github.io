Kuiyi (Cory) Gao

Undergraduate · University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

I work on multimodal safety and the reliability of computer-use agents. My recent projects study how models interpret image–text inputs, how agents handle changes to an interface, and how to monitor third-party agent skills.

Before UNC, I studied at CUHK and worked with Tencent AI Lab. I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Poetry and image variation

ENGE3940 · CUHK · 2025

I compared images generated from ten Imagist and Romantic poems using CLIP-based variation scores. The project examines how prompt length and model choice affect the comparison, and what these measurements can say about literary interpretation.

An exploratory Python prototype for comparing sentence-embedding distributions with RBF kernels and spectral entropy, including centering and whitening.
