Cory Kuiyi Gao

I am an undergraduate researcher at UNC Chapel Hill working on AI safety, alignment, robustness, and AI lawmaking.

My work explains the mechanisms behind vulnerabilities in advanced AI systems (agents, generative models, and memory systems) and develops evaluations, contracts enforced at run time, and alignment methods that make these systems safer and more robust. I also study how research results can be turned into lasting evidence, systems, and common practice for AI governance.

I have two papers at ACL 2025 (Main and Findings) and two under review, one as first author (ICLR 2027) and one as co-first author. I graduate in May 2027 and am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Papers

RuntimeTickets: Source-Grounded Security Obligations for Agent Skills

Kuiyi Gao, Xue Liu, Bowei He

Under review at ICLR 2027. First author.

  • Designed a protocol in which a language model proposes task-specific security obligations for a third-party agent skill, model-free code enforces them on every outbound send, and each run leaves a record that an outside auditor can re-derive.
  • Evaluated it on three unmodified agent frameworks against production skill scanners and runtime defenses: no injected send got through and no benign run was blocked; without it, 27.5% of attack runs succeeded.
arXiv

RealGUINoise: An Interactive Cross-Platform Benchmark for GUI Agent Robustness under Real-World Interface Noise

Yongjiang Wu*, Junyuan Zhang*, Ada Chen*, Kuiyi Gao*, Wenxuan Wang

Under review at ACL Rolling Review. Co-first author (*equal contribution).

  • Constructed benchmark data and built the LLM-judge pipeline, which agrees with human labels in over 94% of cases.
  • Ran the main experiments on 207 web, desktop, and mobile tasks with 42 interface-noise types: across 7 agent frameworks, noise lowered task success by 32.9% and safety by 14.8% (mean relative drop).
arXiv

Chain-of-Jailbreak Attack for Image Generation Models via Step by Step Editing

Wenxuan Wang, Kuiyi Gao, Youliang Yuan, Jen-tse Huang, Qiuzhi Liu, Shuai Wang, Wenxiang Jiao, Zhaopeng Tu

Findings of ACL 2025. Second author.

  • Designed an attack that splits a refused request into harmless-looking editing steps; it bypassed four image generation services in over 60% of cases, versus 14% for other jailbreak methods.
  • Built CoJ-Bench and the Think-Twice Prompting defense, which blocks over 95% of these attacks.

Can't See the Forest for the Trees: Benchmarking Multimodal Safety Awareness for Multimodal LLMs

Wenxuan Wang, Xiaoyuan Liu, Kuiyi Gao, Jen-tse Huang, Youliang Yuan, Pinjia He, Shuai Wang, Zhaopeng Tu

ACL 2025 Main. Third author.

  • Constructed image-text pairs for the benchmark (1,500 pairs, 29 safety scenarios); on it, GPT-4V judged 36.1% of unsafe inputs safe and 59.9% of benign inputs unsafe.
  • Ran vision-centric reasoning fine-tuning, one of the three mitigations the paper tests.

CUHK Summer Undergraduate Research Programme, with Prof. Farzan Farnia, 2025
