    MemSlot: saliency for long contracts

    COMP 586 · UNC · 2026

    I implemented learnable memory-slot attention over a frozen RoBERTa encoder. The scores guide text selection and document rendering; a separate pipeline passes OCR-decoded text to a QA reader. The project compares supervised and unsupervised saliency and includes text-pruning baselines. End-to-end evaluation remains unfinished.

    code & notebooks

  • Original course-project samples.

    Poetry and image variation

    ENGE3940 · CUHK · 2025

    I compared images generated from ten Imagist and Romantic poems with FLUX and Stable Diffusion. I used CLIP-based variation scores, examined prompt-length effects, and compared the poem-level scores statistically. The small sample and unresolved duplicate scores limit the conclusions about literary interpretation.

  • Text-KEN: novelty in text embeddings

    CUHK summer research · Prof. Farzan Farnia · 2025

    I built a numerical prototype for comparing sentence-embedding distributions with RBF kernels and spectral entropy, and explored centering and whitening.

  • Movability segmentation

    ELEG5491 · CUHK · 2025

    A DeepLabV3 project that groups COCO-Stuff labels into four movability classes, with a custom ASPP head.
