Kuiyi (Cory) Gao

I am an undergraduate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. I study the safety of models that work with images and text, and the reliability of agents that use web and mobile interfaces.

Before UNC, I studied at the Chinese University of Hong Kong and worked with Tencent AI Lab. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Ongoing research

RealGUINoise: GUI agents under interface changes

CUHK · Prof. Michael R. Lyu · co-first-authored manuscript under review

We test how agents respond when web and Android interfaces change, for example through overlays or moving content. I designed metrics for task completion, unsafe actions, and changes in action sequences, and helped implement paired tests on original and altered interfaces.

Runtime safety for agent skills

MBZUAI UGRIP · Dr. Steve Liu · continuing research

I built software that monitors what an agent skill does and compares it with the skill’s declared behavior, as part of a four-student UGRIP team. My continuing research studies permission checks and records that let a reviewer inspect why an action was allowed or blocked.

MemSlot: selecting text from long contracts

COMP 586 · UNC · 2026

I built a model that scores words in long contracts to guide text selection and page layout for OCR. The recognized text goes to a separate question-answering model; evaluation of the full pipeline is not yet complete.

Poetry and image variation

ENGE3940 · CUHK · 2025

For each poem excerpt, I analyzed how much the generated images differ. I compared saved CLIP-based scores across ten Imagist and Romantic poems for FLUX and Stable Diffusion.

Text-KEN: comparing collections of text

CUHK summer research · Prof. Farzan Farnia · 2025

I built a prototype for comparing two collections of text through their sentence embeddings. It uses RBF kernels and spectral entropy; I also explored centering and whitening.

Movability segmentation

ELEG5491 · CUHK · 2025

A DeepLabV3 segmentation project with a custom ASPP head. It groups COCO-Stuff labels into four movability classes, from fixed structures to people and vehicles.
