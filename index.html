Kuiyi (Cory) Gao

I am an undergraduate at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, expecting to graduate in May 2027. My research focuses on AI safety and auditable AI deployment.

I have worked on multimodal safety with Tencent AI Lab, GUI-agent robustness at CUHK, and runtime monitoring during MBZUAI UGRIP. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Research interests

For my PhD, I want to study how AI systems handle unsafe inputs, changing environments, and conflicting human values. I am interested in how we can evaluate and improve their safety, and give people the evidence and control needed to oversee important actions.

Ongoing research

RealGUINoise: GUI agents under interface changes

CUHK · Prof. Michael R. Lyu · co-first-authored manuscript under review

We test agents on paired web and Android tasks with original and altered interfaces. My work: dataset construction, evaluation methodology, the automated judging pipeline, and experiments. I designed measures of task completion, unsafe actions, and changes in action sequences.

RuntimeTickets: runtime safety for agent skills

MBZUAI UGRIP and continuing research · Dr. Steve Liu · first-author manuscript under review at ICLR 2027

I built the runtime monitor in a four-student UGRIP team and now lead RuntimeTickets, including method development, experiments, and writing. The work checks agent actions against approved permissions and records the observations behind each safety decision.

Text-KEN: comparing collections of text

CUHK summer research · Prof. Farzan Farnia · 2025

I built a prototype for comparing two collections of text through their sentence embeddings. It uses RBF kernels and spectral entropy; I also explored centering and whitening.

Movability segmentation

ELEG5491 · CUHK · 2025

A DeepLabV3 segmentation project with a custom ASPP head. It groups COCO-Stuff labels into four movability classes, from fixed structures to people and vehicles.
