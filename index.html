I am an undergraduate at UNC-Chapel Hill, graduating in May 2027. I study AI safety through evaluation and runtime controls, with work on multimodal models and GUI agents.

Research

RealGUINoise: A Dynamic Cross-Platform Benchmark for GUI Agent Robustness under Real-World Interface Noise

Co-first author. Under review.

Each web and Android task is paired with an altered interface so robustness can be measured against the same goal. As co-first author, I built tasks, designed the protocol and automated judge, and ran the paired experiments.

RuntimeTickets: Enforcing and Auditing Task-Specific Security Requirements for Third-Party Agent Skills

First author. Under review at ICLR 2027.

RuntimeTickets turns a task into a narrow permission contract, then records each runtime decision and effect. As first author, I designed the method, built the firewall with the UGRIP team, and lead experiments and writing.

Can't See the Forest for the Trees: Benchmarking Multimodal Safety Awareness for Multimodal LLMs

Wenxuan Wang, Xiaoyuan Liu, Kuiyi Gao, Jen-tse Huang, Youliang Yuan, Pinjia He, Shuai Wang, Zhaopeng Tu

ACL 2025

The benchmark measures missed unsafe inputs and benign false alarms separately. As third author, I contributed data, evaluation, vision-centric fine-tuning, submission, and rebuttal.

Chain-of-Jailbreak Attack for Image Generation Models via Step by Step Editing

Wenxuan Wang, Kuiyi Gao, Youliang Yuan, Jen-tse Huang, Qiuzhi Liu, Shuai Wang, Wenxiang Jiao, Zhaopeng Tu

Findings of ACL 2025

The study tests safety failures that emerge during multi-turn image editing. As second author, I designed the attack and defense, contributed data, ran most experiments, and prepared figures, writing, and rebuttal.

Projects

MemSlot: selecting text from long contracts

Natural language processing course project, UNC

MemSlot tests whether learned saliency can compress contracts before OCR and question answering. I implemented attention slots over frozen RoBERTa features, overlap-aware scoring, saliency-guided rendering, and pruning baselines.

Code and notebooksOutput

Poetry and image variation

AI and language course project, CUHK

This project compares image variation across Imagist and Romantic poems. I generated images with FLUX and Stable Diffusion and analyzed saved CLIP-based scores with SciPy and Matplotlib.

Generated images

Text-KEN: comparing collections of text

Summer research with Prof. Farzan Farnia, CUHK

Text-KEN compares two text collections without training a classifier. I built a MiniLM embedding pipeline with RBF kernels, spectral entropy, centering, and covariance whitening.

Movability segmentation

Deep learning course project, CUHK

This project maps scene categories into four movability classes. I adapted DeepLabV3 with a custom ASPP head and implemented COCO-Stuff remapping, image-mask preprocessing, and checkpoint inference.
