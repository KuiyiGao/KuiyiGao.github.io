# Figure sources

The homepage uses original paper figures and saved project outputs.

- `figs/mmsafeaware-figure3.png`: Figure 3, page 12 of [Wang et al., ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.832.pdf). Cropped from the published PDF; axes, legend, panels and caption preserved. CC BY 4.0.
- `figs/coj-figure3.png`: Figure 3, page 6 of [Wang et al., Findings of ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.571.pdf). Cropped from the published PDF; axes, legend and caption preserved. CC BY 4.0.
- `figs/memslot-saliency.png`: Original PNG embedded in cell 6 (zero-based) of [MemSlot_Report.ipynb](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/Distorted-OCR-Compression/blob/b48f11cf7cdc05d194aa6bd33ec15369d654dfc1/notebooks/MemSlot_Report.ipynb). Saved course-project output, extracted without modification. The models were not rerun for this website refresh.
- `figs/poetry-samples.jpg`: Two archived FLUX image grids from Kuiyi Gao's ENGE3940 project (2025), arranged with labels for the homepage. These selected examples are illustrative; they do not establish a measured difference between literary movements.

The original research reports contain additional figures that are not presented here as verified results.
