RuntimeTickets: Source-Grounded Security Obligations for Agent Skills

ICLR 2027 under review, first author; with Prof. Xue (Steve) Liu and Bowei He (MBZUAI and McGill).

  • Designed a protocol in which a language model proposes task-specific security obligations for a third-party agent skill, model-free code enforces them on every outbound send, and each run leaves a record that an outside auditor can re-derive.
  • Evaluated it on three unmodified agent frameworks against production skill scanners and runtime defenses: no injected send got through and no benign run was blocked; without it, 27.5% of attack runs succeeded.
arXiv

RealGUINoise: GUI-Agent Robustness under Real-World Interface Noise

Under review, co-first author; with Prof. Wenxuan Wang (Renmin University of China).

  • Constructed benchmark data and built the LLM-judge pipeline, which agrees with human labels in over 94% of cases.
  • Ran the main experiments on 207 web, desktop, and mobile tasks with 42 interface-noise types: across 7 agent frameworks, noise lowered task success by 32.9% and safety by 14.8% (mean relative drop).
arXiv

Chain-of-Jailbreak: Jailbreaking Image Generation Models by Step-by-Step Editing

Findings of ACL 2025, second author; with Prof. Wenxuan Wang and Dr. Zhaopeng Tu. Tencent AI Lab and CUHK.

  • Designed an attack that splits a refused request into harmless-looking editing steps; it bypassed four image generation services in over 60% of cases, versus 14% for other jailbreak methods.
  • Built CoJ-Bench and the Think-Twice Prompting defense, which blocks over 95% of these attacks.

MMSafeAware: A Benchmark for Multimodal Safety Awareness

ACL 2025 Main, third author; with Prof. Wenxuan Wang and Dr. Zhaopeng Tu. Tencent AI Lab.

  • Constructed image-text pairs for the benchmark (1,500 pairs, 29 safety scenarios); on it, GPT-4V judged 36.1% of unsafe inputs safe and 59.9% of benign inputs unsafe.
  • Ran vision-centric reasoning fine-tuning, one of the three mitigations the paper tests.
