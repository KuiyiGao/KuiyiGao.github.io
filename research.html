RealGUINoise

Co-first author. Under review.

What changes when a GUI agent meets a popup, delayed content, or a look-alike control? We run the same web and Android tasks on clean and altered interfaces. I built tasks, browser automation, the automated judge, and the paired evaluation of completion, unsafe actions, and action sequences.

arXiv

RuntimeTickets

First author. Under review at ICLR 2027.

RuntimeTickets checks agent tool use before execution and records PASS, BLOCK, or DEFER decisions together with the observed effect. I designed the task-scoped contract, built the runtime layer and reviewable traces with the UGRIP team, and lead the follow-up experiments and manuscript.

MMSafeAware

Third author. ACL 2025.

The benchmark asks whether multimodal models notice risks in images without treating harmless inputs as unsafe. As third author, I contributed data and evaluation, ran vision-centric fine-tuning and error analysis, and worked on submission and rebuttal.

Chain-of-Jailbreak

Second author. Findings of ACL 2025.

The study looks at safety failures that emerge over several image-editing turns. As second author, I designed the attack and prompting defense, contributed data, ran most experiments, and prepared figures, writing, and rebuttal.
