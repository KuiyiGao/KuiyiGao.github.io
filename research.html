Work on safety evaluation and control for multimodal and agentic systems.

RealGUINoise

Co-first author. Under review.

RealGUINoise evaluates GUI agents on paired web and Android tasks before and after realistic interface changes. I built benchmark tasks, designed the evaluation protocol, implemented automated judging, and ran the paired experiments for task completion, unsafe actions, and action-sequence differences.

RuntimeTickets

First author. Under review at ICLR 2027.

RuntimeTickets enforces task-specific contracts that narrow the permissions available to third-party agent skills. I designed the method, built the runtime firewall with the UGRIP team, and lead the experiments and manuscript.

MMSafeAware

Third author. ACL 2025.

The study tests whether models classify an image and its accompanying text as safe or unsafe, separately measuring missed risks and false alarms. I contributed to benchmark construction and evaluation, ran vision-centric fine-tuning experiments, and worked on submission and rebuttal.

Chain-of-Jailbreak

Second author. Findings of ACL 2025.

Chain-of-Jailbreak studies unsafe image generation that emerges across multi-turn editing. I designed the method and prompting-based defense, contributed benchmark data, ran most attack and defense experiments, and prepared figures, writing, and rebuttal.
