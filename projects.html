MemSlot: saliency for long contracts

COMP 586 · UNC · 2026

Learnable memory slots attend to frozen RoBERTa features to estimate word-level saliency. Selected text is rendered as a document image, decoded with OCR, and passed as text to a separate QA reader. End-to-end QA evaluation is ongoing.

Poetry and image variation

ENGE3940 · CUHK · 2025

The project compares images generated from ten Imagist and Romantic poems using FLUX and Stable Diffusion. My analysis uses CLIP-based variation scores to compare the two groups and examine the relationship between prompt length and image variation.
